HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag anywhere in the bottom band (or use ← → ↑ ↓) to move 106 — sparks fire on their own.
  • The Sparkpede winds down through the mushrooms. Shoot a segment and it becomes a mushroom, splitting the chain — heads score most.
  • Mushrooms take 4 hits. They steer the pede and clutter your band.
  • Watch the spider (roams your band), the flea (drops straight down) and the scorpion (poisons mushrooms — a poisoned mushroom sends the pede plunging).
  • Clear the whole pede for a faster, longer wave. It speeds up every wave and never stops — climb the leaderboard by score.
